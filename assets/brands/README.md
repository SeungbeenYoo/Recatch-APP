# Card issuer logo sources

Original public assets from issuer sites, displayed in their original proportions on a white background without recoloring or shape edits.

- `kb-logo.png`: [KB header image](https://img1.kbcard.com/LT/images_r/common/kbcard_Logo_v3.png), referenced by [official header CSS](https://card.kbcard.com/CMN/common/pc/css/header_new.css). [CI guidance](https://card.kbcard.com/SVC/DVIEW/HSJMCXCROCIC0025).
- `sh-logo.png`: [Shinhan Korean signature](https://www.shinhancard.com/pconts/company/images/contents/shc_ci_basic_00.png), from [CI guidance](https://www.shinhancard.com/pconts/company/html/promotion/CI/ci_basic.html).
- `hd-logo.svg`: [Hyundai Card header SVG](https://www.hyundaicard.com/docfiles/resources/pc/images/common/logo/logo_HC.svg), referenced by [official layout CSS](https://www.hyundaicard.com/docfiles/resources/pc/css/layout.css).

Downloaded on 2026-10-07. Trademarks belong to their issuers. Their presence identifies a card issuer in the prototype and does not assert a partnership or verified product terms.
